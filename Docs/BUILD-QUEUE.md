# Shift-click construction queue

Shift + left click appends a tower order; a normal build click replaces the current queue. Orders retain their chosen tower design and complete in click order, not nearest-first order. The compact tower bar now shows the pending count and Shift-click hint. Cancel Orders or moving the builder cancels the queue; opening the Esc menu preserves and pauses it.

On reaching construction range, the builder revalidates the footprint, prerequisites and wallet. If an enemy has moved into the footprint, or another condition prevents building, that order is discarded without charging and the builder proceeds to the next. It does not wait or retry skipped orders. Existing invalid placement is rejected when clicked without deleting previously accepted orders. Orders are paid only on success, so a queue does not reserve funds. Failure feedback is explicitly prefixed “Skipped order” in the detailed builder status.

The existing FIFO and failed-order advancement were already implemented. This update exposes the queue count in the compact HUD, clarifies failure feedback and adds regression coverage. Unity compilation completed without errors. All 63 pure simulation cases passed, including an enemy placed into the middle footprint after accepting three orders, far-to-near ordering, no charge for the blocked order, continuation, no later retry, and exhaustion of an unreserved wallet. Existing design-retention, duplicate and cancellation coverage also passed. Package checkpoints remain those recorded in Howl-Builds.json; no new packaged-input pass is claimed for this change.
