# Living orders, unmistakable defenders

2026-10-10. The player asked for creature factions and an entire order of different ancient trees that throw seeds and saplings. This expands the art direction: a defensive unit may be a creature, a person, a spirit or a crafted instrument. “Tower” describes its gameplay function, not a requirement to draw a turret.

Keep the cozy, inhabited world, expressive faces and powerful large shapes. Our guardians defend a home. A recognizable personality at ordinary playing distance matters more than piles of small ornaments.

## Rootbound — first living family

Rootbound replaces the former Stonebound presentation on Rimewatch. Its faction/design IDs, costs, damage, targeting, upgrades, health, build footprints and blocking behavior stay the same. Its defenders are rooted in their assigned cells, with original heartwood profiles, leaf meshes, faces, crowns and branch arms. They breathe and throw according to simulation ticks, so pause and match speed govern them.

| Defender | Creature | Attack / function |
|---|---|---|
| Seedling Warden (formerly Pebble Warden) | Young forked hazel, bright eyes, broad leaf ears | Small acorn volleys, ground and air |
| Oldbark (formerly Basalt Ward) | Broad old oak with folded arms and a sheltered acorn nook | Durable living maze wall; no attack |
| Briar Elder (formerly Burr Elder / Quake Idol) | Twisted hawthorn, exposed thorn crown and a heavy throwing bough | Barbed thorn clusters with ground splash |
| Skybough (formerly Crag Hurler) | Narrow upright pine with raised branch fingers | Winged seeds aimed at flying enemies |
| Worldroot | Old willow, root beard and hanging leaf curtains | Sapling-shaped bursts with the existing slowing ground impact |

Saplings are cosmetic projectiles in this pass. They do not create allied units, extra route blockers or additional damage. The portraits and queued build previews use the actual tree models. Original seeds share the existing capped single-renderer projectile path. Branch motion retains a margin inside the occupied cell.

The table below is the target for subsequent model work, **not a claim that all of it is implemented**. Rootbound is the first new creature pass. Pulse's workshop pass and the preceding faction silhouettes remain the current art elsewhere.

Crafted defenses remain welcome. Their stone, wood, copper and cloth must visibly belong to the same settlements as the living defenders. Creature factions should not become ordinary cannon barrels with eyes attached.

## Review each family in the actual game

1. Recognize the family and each role at ordinary camera zoom, in a mixed defense and in small portraits.
2. Connect attacks to anatomy: a beak, throwing arm, seed hand, opened shell or breath. Keep target readability and distinct projectile colors.
3. Give each creature restrained idle motion and a clear action pose; freeze simulation-driven movement while paused.
4. Keep every visible animated pose, including level three and diagonal aiming, inside its build cell. The maze must remain readable and wall-tight.
5. Preserve the fixed team economy, paid construction, ownership, multiplayer authority, map masks and existing role balance. Test any mechanical expansion separately.
6. Update the actual builder, selection description, portraits, preview and material sounds as a coherent family. Record what is implemented versus remaining.

The current procedural models are an iteration toward this direction, not finished sculpted character art. Do not import likenesses or assets from reference games or films.
