# Faction identity charter

## Current implementation — 2026-10-10

The user’s six requested themes are present: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Tech and Magic. The other six are Nature, Ice, Storm, Beasts, Dragons and Spirits. [The elemental roster](ELEMENTAL-FACTIONS.md) is the source of truth for current names, map availability and body forms.

All twelve now have a complete procedural visual family: 64 creature/spirit replacements, five Rootbound trees and seven Pulse workshop mechanisms. Theme labels lead the selector. Shared role mechanics and numeric roster IDs remain intact. This completes the first family pass, not final sculpted art; builders still use their prior wardwright anatomy with new palettes.

Every defender must remain recognizable within its family at playing distance. Distinguish body form, stance, height, negative space and dominant weapon or appendage. Color and tiny accessories cannot carry recognition alone. Keep owner/upgrade cues legible and connect the projectile to the creature’s anatomy.

## Review each family in the actual game

1. Inspect normal zoom, a mixed defense, small portraits and two-angle color-free silhouettes.
2. Connect attacks to a mouth, beak, throwing arm, opened shell or spell; keep projectile colors readable.
3. Give creatures restrained idle movement and an action pose; freeze simulation-driven movement in pause.
4. Keep animated level-three and diagonal poses inside the actual build cell. Add no decorative collision.
5. Preserve the fixed team economy, paid construction, ownership, authority, masks and roles. Mechanical expansions need separate balance work.
6. Carry family identity into keepers, previews, descriptions and material sounds, documenting unfinished anatomy and listening checks.

Original art only. League, Dota and the classic mauls inform warmth, clarity and world cohesion; do not import their assets or character likenesses.
