# Howl for Maul — world and art bible

## The promise: warmth worth defending

At the edge of a long winter, small settlements survive around hearth-forges and wardbells. The Howl wakes what sleeps beneath snow, stone and slag. Rival orders bring their strange machines and old magic to the same road: the road home.

You are a wardwright. Your towers are the work of a living order, assembled into a defense with friends. Last Stand is the threshold of a refuge, not an abstract finish line. Keep the hearth lit.

This is new Howl for Maul fiction. It does not claim to be lore from Warcraft, Mega Man, League or Dota. The maul inheritance is cooperative freeform mazing, simultaneous lanes, escalating waves and expressive faction rosters.

## Places

| Place | Character | Shape / material | Light / sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ironfold | A reclaimed foundry town; life growing around old industry | Broad slate roads, chamfered masonry, copper roofs, patinated brass, ivy and scrub; the paired Anvilheart halls shelter Last Stand | Cool green shade with amber hearths; low forge breath, wind through pipes, measured wardbells |
| Rimewatch | A mountain sanctuary keeping its welcome through deep winter | Blue slate, heavy timber, layered snow and sheltering pines; paired Hearthward lodges | Soft snow fill, warm windows and blue distance; pine wind, quiet hearth crackle, clear bells |

Landmarks belong to these places: a forge needs a chimney, fuel and worn stone; a refuge needs windows, a roof, a road and shelter. Sparse clusters with purpose take precedence over evenly scattered ornaments. Left and right remain mirrored. The exit approach stays open and its back walls flat.

## The Howl

Ground attackers are slagbound creatures: armored bodies around a dangerous inner glow. Runners have a narrow, swept silhouette; breakers have broad frontal plates; flying drifters have unmistakable wings and a suspended body. Eyes, breathing and footfall give them intention. Threat readability takes priority over decorative detail.

## Visual grammar

- Read from the normal playing camera first; close-up detail is a reward.
- Broad painted surfaces, deliberate chipped edges, restrained noise. Avoid a field of tiny high-contrast objects.
- Cold surroundings and warm inhabited pockets. Emission belongs to fire, wards and active weapons.
- Roads remain flat and fully buildable according to the map mask. Decoration never creates a hidden collider, reservation or routing rule.
- New buildings stay beyond the playable boundary or entirely on already blocked terrain. No airborne silhouettes hidden by tall props.
- Use original meshes, textures and symbols. References establish quality and mood, not copied assets.
- Cinzel for short ceremonial headings; Alegreya Sans for readable instructions and numbers. Keep long gameplay labels legible.
- Attacks are material sounds (stone, metal, air, glass, heat); UI is a soft mechanical response. Important leaks have a clear wardbell. Music leaves space for play.

## Selected visual target

The [Ironfold blank-canvas concept](Concept-Target/Ironfold-Visual-Target.png) is the user-selected composition and material target. See [the implementation direction](CONCEPT-TO-WORLD.md). Match its sheltering woodland, rooted stone and warm refuge architecture while preserving the supplied lane geometry and every buildable cell. The concept is not a replacement map mask or evidence of implemented quality.

## First finished-area target

Ironfold Last Stand: painted slate road, readable wall edges, paired Anvilheart halls beyond the exit, warm apertures, layered roof silhouettes and a clear central path. Validate with ordinary paid towers and moving enemies, not only an empty overview. Apply the same construction language to Rimewatch's Hearthward lodges.

## Acceptance and limits

Maintain all-active lanes, fixed four-player team economy, paid construction, precise tower-to-wall closure, faction mechanics, camera controls and north-up minimap. No mobile or scheduler work. Evaluate visual changes in packaged Linux captures at normal zoom and at 960×600 as well as 1440×900. Run simulation regressions and multiplayer probes. Native Windows and a full match with friends on separate networks remain external playtest gates; automated local tests cannot substitute for them.
