# Rime Covenant model slice

This pass develops one original faction toward the requested restrained, League-inspired direction. It is an in-engine model prototype, not a claim of finished commercial art quality.

The five Rime Covenant towers now use stone plinths, pale trim and smaller blue ice accents, with distinct silhouettes: a crenellated shard launcher, a stacked snow cairn, a tall control crystal held by stone claws, a low open artillery basin, and paired anti-air spires. Existing faction colors, upgrade markers, shot-facing and recoil remain. Other factions retain their earlier role designs.

Winter builders are compact hooded wardens with faction-colored mantles and crystal staffs. Ironfold builders are small faceted drones with swept stabilizers. Their dark structural materials stay separate from faction tint. Their flying builder behavior, travel speed, build range and construction costs are unchanged.

Enemies use faceted shells and swept wing meshes. Ground feet animate while moving using simulation ticks, so pause and setup freeze the motion. Existing orange ground / purple air identities, heavy shields, runner fins, slow markers and siege colors remain. This is still a shared enemy family, not a completed biome-specific bestiary.

Original profile and wing meshes are shared within a map and released when it unloads. Presentation meshes add no collision authority. No source mask, placement grid, navigation rule, tower statistic, enemy radius or economy value changed.

Verification extends the existing Play-mode tests: distinct Rime model parts, shared tower/wing meshes, map-unload cleanup, builder material separation, no cosmetic colliders, and walking animation freeze/resume. Existing role, upgrade, firing, setup, collision and ownership tests remain required. Screenshots are controlled paid-tower fixtures with staged enemies, not balance playthroughs or target-hardware performance benchmarks.

Remaining visual work: bespoke designs for the other factions, more expressive enemy bodies and attacks, softer environmental transitions and richer original materials. Keep comparing at normal gameplay zoom before expanding detail.
