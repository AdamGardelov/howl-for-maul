# Seven engines of Blast Circuit

2026-10-10. Blast Circuit's role silhouettes now come from pressure engines rather than a repeated chassis with a small weapon attached.

- Alloy Cadet: split copper shell plates around a low forward cannon.
- Crashbreaker: an open press frame, raised hammer, guide rods and visible anvil.
- Gale Pilot: a circular five-vane pressure fan on a low cradle.
- Heatkeeper: a tall banded pressure kettle with a gauge, paired flues, steam nozzles and furnace grille.
- Quicksilver: raised swept wings, paired sky-bomb rails and a sight, retaining its air-only splash role.
- Rootguard: a low shielded three-barrel battery with splayed bores and elevation gears.
- Citadel Champion: a broad armored prow, paired siege mortars and a high split standard.

The order retains its copper bodies, violet fittings/core light and six articulated legs. A smaller undercarriage replaces the repeated bulky shell/treads; weapon heights are no longer compressed into that same chassis. Dark bores and collars separate gun mouths from solid rods. The actual-model portrait and placement-preview systems inherit every change.

Original repository-authored geometry only; no new asset downloads or copied reference designs. Rigid pieces still combine into shared per-design meshes. Costs, names, damage, targeting, projectiles, sound signatures, upgrades, prerequisites, ownership, map masks, economy and online flow remain unchanged. No extra lights, colliders, per-frame mesh generation or new materials per tower are added. Upgraded, diagonally aimed models retain the original occupied-cell envelope.

See [verification and in-game images](Verification/Blast-Engines/README.md). These are more distinct production models, not a claim of finished commercial character art. Other rosters, player bodies and richer attack animation remain useful work.
