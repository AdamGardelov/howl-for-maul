# Siege readability

Actual ground melee attacks now record a presentation timestamp and direction. A crawler faces its target and tilts briefly on impact; the pose uses simulation ticks and freezes during pause/setup. It does not translate the enemy root or change its collision disc, movement, attack cadence or damage.

A rendered tower losing health emits a short red outline around its footprint. A destroyed rendered tower leaves a brief original gray rubble burst, explaining the newly opened route. Healthy tower sales and match resets do not emit destruction effects. Like existing enemy-removal effects, a tower created and removed wholly between view updates has no removal view to emit a cue.

These effects share the existing 64-object budget. Destruction can replace the oldest effect at capacity; nonfatal impact is dropped at capacity. Rubble uses one cached mesh per map, has no collider and casts no shadows. Effects follow game speed, freeze during pause/setup and clear on a new match. No new sound was added.
