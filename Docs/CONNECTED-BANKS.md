# Connected Ironfold banks

2026-10-10. Continues the [selected concept direction](CONCEPT-TO-WORLD.md). Ironfold's flat blocked shelves now rise into connected, irregular mossy ridges, with exposed natural slate on their steeper sides. This replaces the impression of separate rock bases placed on a flat board with larger landscape forms. The existing exact retaining edges stay readable beside buildable lanes.

The original mask supplies all admission decisions. Shared mesh vertices sit at blocked cell centres, and each complete quad requires four original `#` cells. Heights taper beside navigable terrain, water, forge aprons and fire anchors. Near flying routes the ground remains below the existing visual-clearance limit. Both halves meet on the exact centre plane and reflect the same vertices, normals and texture coordinates. Nothing is added to occupancy, physics, economy or route rules.

The first render used separate meadow/stone quads and exposed obvious tile boundaries. That version was rejected. The retained surface blends continuously between the existing baked shelf paint and new original bedrock, with smooth shared normals and world-space texture projection on steep faces. Reusing the existing shelf paint also keeps the new slopes from turning into bright green patches. A subsequent close-up exposed a lighting mismatch at the tapered foot, so the bank shader uses the same URP PBR lighting and roughness as the original shelf, rather than the painted-leaf lighting. The new slate replaces reused paving on the preceding bank outcrops. One additional static terrain batch is used; no terrain mesh uploads or placement work occur each frame.

`HearthBedrock.png` was generated specifically for this project, copied unchanged, and inspected before integration. Its source, complete prompt and importer settings are recorded in [the asset manifest](CONNECTED-BANKS-ASSET.json). It uses a maximum 1024 import, mipmaps and no CPU-readable copy. No reference-game assets are included. The generated texture is a material asset, not a picture substituted for gameplay geometry.

This pass is deliberately Ironfold-specific. Rimewatch retains its snowy shapes and remains a regression control. Current geometry is still procedural: retaining-wall stair steps, sparse/repeated tree crowns, small settlement models and character detail remain below the chosen concept's quality. The connected landform is a foundation for further woodland composition, not a claim of matching that image or reaching paid-release readiness.

See [verification and actual packaged captures](Verification/Connected-Banks/README.md). Preserve the broad forms when refining trees and their roots; avoid covering the open refuge approach or narrowing a buildable lane to make a prettier screenshot.
