# Wardwrights who belong to their orders

2026-10-10. Three Ironfold builders now have purposeful grounded bodies and working tools instead of the common hovering robot beneath a faction crest.

Pulse Foundry has a slim enamel mechanic, separated boots, a signal aerial, precision driver and folded plans. Blast Circuit has a squat copper smith with a face shield and goggles, apron, paired pressure cylinders, hammer and tongs. Horizon Guild has a hooded surveyor with a cloak, monocle, packed tripod, hand scope and field tablet. Their hands and tools use the existing paid-completion gesture; walking turns and strides follow authoritative simulation ticks and freeze on pause. These three no longer bob while standing on the road.

All eight Ironfold builders are more compact: a maximum 80% scale, further limited by their complete neutral tool/body envelope. The scale is anchored at the ground, preserving feet and visual contact rather than lifting the model upward. Ownership rings retain their original size, owner color and ground height. The other five Ironfold orders retain their existing hover bodies and distinct crests for now. Rimewatch's four wardens are unchanged.

Parts still use the existing original shared mesh/material library. Bodies and articulated limbs batch into cached per-order meshes at setup, with no extra materials, lights, colliders or per-frame mesh generation. This changes no build distance, movement speed, queue behavior, economy, map mask, ownership rule, networking or audio. No downloaded reference assets are introduced; Scott Buckley attribution remains intact.

[Verification and screenshots](Verification/Wardwright-Builders/README.md) cover all twelve builders and paid work by each new body. The remaining hover bodies and more expressive character animation are useful follow-ups; these are production improvements, not a claim that the full commercial art bar is complete.
